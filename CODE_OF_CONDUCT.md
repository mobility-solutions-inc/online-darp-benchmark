# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, safe, and inclusive project where people can
disagree about technical and scientific questions without personal hostility.
This policy applies in repository spaces, project events, and private
communications made in a project role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal attributes or motives.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give specific, constructive review and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and respect confidentiality.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and research integrity.
- Stop conduct when asked by a moderator and cooperate with resolution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, plagiarism, result manipulation,
retaliation, and publication of private benchmark information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For routine moderation, contact a maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). For a private report, use Ontra Mobility's
private contact method listed there. Do not open a public issue if doing so could
expose a target or sensitive information.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports, limit disclosure to people needed for a
fair review, hear affected parties where safe, document conflicts, and respond in
proportion to impact and recurrence. Responses may include a private warning,
content removal, temporary restriction, removal from the project, or referral to
the relevant platform or authorities. Retaliation against a reporter or witness
is itself a violation.

If a report concerns a maintainer, that person is excluded from handling it. If
no unconflicted maintainer is available, the organization will designate an
independent reviewer.
