## Contact

- Technical and governance questions: use a GitHub issue or discussion.
- Hidden instance intake: use the public metadata-minimal **Hidden instance
  intake request** issue form. If even the submitter identity must remain
  confidential, contact Ontra Mobility at <https://www.ontramobility.com/> and
  identify the message as an Online DARP Benchmark hidden-instance submission.
- Security vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Private conduct reports: contact Ontra Mobility through the private contact
  method published at <https://www.ontramobility.com/> and identify the report as
  concerning this repository.

Maintainer appointment, recusal, and removal rules are defined in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
