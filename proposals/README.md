# Proposals

Benchmark-semantic changes are developed as numbered RFCs under the process in
[GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md). A proposal is not part of the official
benchmark contract until its status is changed to Accepted and the corresponding
versioned schemas and validator are merged.
